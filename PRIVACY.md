## 🏛️ 1. Privacy Philosophy

At **AirBorne**, we design intelligence systems and HRMS platforms with **Privacy by Design** as a non-negotiable core standard. We believe software should operate transparently without stealth telemetry, secret user tracking, or unauthorized training on proprietary data.

---

## 📊 2. Telemetry & Analytics Principles

1. **Zero Silent Telemetry:** Open-source libraries and algorithms (such as `Airborne-Antara` and `Numpy-Transformer`) contain **zero telemetry, zero analytics tracking, and zero outbound network phoning**.
2. **Local Execution by Default:** All model training, inference, and memory consolidation happen strictly locally on your compute substrate.
3. **No Training on Customer Data:** Enterprise HRMS systems do not utilize private employee records, salaries, or internal documents to train public shared models.

---

## 🛡️ 3. Data Protection & Compliance Guardrails

- **Cryptographic Encryption:** AES-256 GCM encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit for all enterprise microservices.
- **Role-Based PII Scoping:** Compliance with Level 1 through Level 6 zero-trust access control matrices (as specified in `ACCESS_CONTROL.md`).
- **Right to Erasure & Export:** Comprehensive data export and complete cryptographic erasure support for all employee and organizational profiles.
