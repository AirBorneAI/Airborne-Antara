**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Governing Body:** AirBorne Open Source & Engineering Ethics Committee

## 🌌 1. Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, maintainers, and leaders of the **AirBorne** and **AirBorneAI** open-source ecosystem pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free, intellectually enriching, and collaborative experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and psychologically safe engineering community.

---

## ⚡ 2. Core Values

Our community culture is anchored by three core tenets:

1. **Precision & Technical Rigor:** We value well-reasoned architectural debates, data-backed technical arguments, and constructive critique of code, architecture, and mathematics.
2. **Elevation & Mentorship:** We actively help newcomers, interns, and junior contributors elevate their skills through constructive, empathetic code reviews and clear documentation.
3. **Integrity & Accountability:** We take responsibility for our code, our AI models, our security disclosures, and our interactions with fellow engineers.

---

## 🛡️ 3. Standards of Behavior

### ✅ Positive Behaviors (Encouraged)

- **Demonstrating Empathy & Kindness:** Treating all community members with professional respect, patience, and consideration.
- **Constructive Technical Discourse:** Focusing on the merits of code, architectural trade-offs, and algorithmic efficiency rather than personal attributes.
- **Graceful Reception of Feedback:** Accepting technical review and critique openly, acknowledging mistakes, and collaborating towards optimal solutions.
- **Clear & Transparent Communication:** Writing informative commit messages, detailed PR descriptions, and courteous issue discussions.
- **Protecting Community Safety:** Upholding user privacy, data security, and ethical AI development guidelines at all times.

### ❌ Unacceptable Behaviors (Prohibited)

- **Harassment & Discrimination:** The use of sexualized language or imagery, unwelcome sexual attention, or advances of any kind.
- **Hostile & Derogatory Communication:** Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal attacks, or political/religious baiting.
- **Public or Private Harassment:** Repeated unwanted contact, intimidation, stalking, or harassment across any GitHub repository, Discord server, or email channel.
- **Doxxing & Privacy Invasions:** Publishing others' private information (such as physical or electronic addresses, phone numbers, or private code) without their explicit, written permission.
- **Malicious Disruption:** Intentional degradation of CI/CD infrastructure, spamming issues or PR queues, or introducing backdoors/malicious payloads.
- **Unethical AI Manipulation:** Introducing harmful training data poisoned to generate hate speech, non-consensual imagery, or security exploits.

## 📋 4. Scope & Jurisdiction

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces managed by **AirBorne** and **AirBorneAI**, including:

## 🚨 5. Incident Reporting & Triage Process

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have any concerns regarding conduct in an AirBorne project, please report it immediately:

### 📧 How to Report
- **Email:** Send a confidential report to **[talent@airbornehrs.in](mailto:talent@airbornehrs.in)**.
- **Subject Line:** `[Conduct Report] Urgent: <Brief Summary>`

### 📄 What to Include
1. **Date, Time & Location:** Direct links to the relevant comment, PR, issue, or commit.
2. **Parties Involved:** GitHub usernames or identifiers of the offending party and any witnesses.
3. **Detailed Description:** A factual summary of the incident and the specific behavior in question.
4. **Context & Screenshots:** Any supporting screenshots, logs, or prior interaction history.

### ⏱️ Response Commitments
- **Initial Acknowledgement:** Within **24 hours** of receipt.
- **Investigation & Committee Review:** Completed within **5 business days**.
- **Formal Resolution & Action:** Delivered confidentially to all affected parties within **7 business days**.

All reports will be handled with strict confidentiality. The committee will not disclose the reporter's identity without explicit consent.

## ⚖️ 6. 4-Tier Enforcement Guidelines & Consequences

Community leaders will follow these four standardized enforcement guidelines to determine fair and proportionate consequences:

### Tier 1: Correction & Informal Guidance
- **Community Impact:** Use of inappropriate language or other minor unprofessional behavior.
- **Consequence:** A private, written reprimand from maintainers providing clarity on why the behavior was unacceptable, accompanied by a request for a public or private apology.

### Tier 2: Formal Warning & Interaction Restriction
- **Community Impact:** A violation through a single serious incident or a continuous pattern of inappropriate behavior.
- **Consequence:** A formal written warning specifying the consequences of continued violations. Temporary restriction from interacting with the community (e.g., 30-day interaction ban on issues/PRs).

### Tier 3: Temporary Suspension
- **Community Impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including sustained harassment or discriminatory remarks.
- **Consequence:** A temporary ban from all communication, contribution, and repository access across the organization for a specified period (e.g., 90 to 180 days).

### Tier 4: Permanent Expulsion
- **Community Impact:** Demonstrating a pattern of violation, doxxing, hate speech, physical threats, intentional introduction of vulnerabilities, or harassment of any individual.
- **Consequence:** A permanent ban from all current and future participation, repositories, and communication channels within the AirBorne organization.

---

## 🛡️ 7. Anti-Retaliation Policy

AirBorne strictly prohibits any form of retaliation against any individual who reports a violation in good faith or participates in an investigation. Retaliatory actions will be treated as a **Tier 4 violation**, resulting in immediate expulsion.
